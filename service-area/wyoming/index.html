Services in Wyoming

Diagnostics, engine, electrical, hydraulic, welding & fabrication, PM service, and emergency repair — on site, statewide. Tap a service for details.

Diagnostics

Warning lights, fault codes, and no-starts chased down with proper scan tools and systematic troubleshooting — not parts swapping.

Engine

Diesel engine repair and overhaul support — hard starts, power loss, overheating, leaks, and in-frame work in the field.

Electrical

Wiring, charging, starting, and control systems. The faults that strand good machines get traced to the wire that's actually broken.

Hydraulic

Pumps, cylinders, valves, and hoses. Weak functions, drift, and leaks diagnosed by pressure and flow — not by feel.

Welding & Fabrication

Field welding and fab: cracked buckets, broken mounts, worn edges, and the custom bracket that doesn't exist in a catalog.

PM Service

Planned maintenance done on your schedule at your site — fluids, filters, inspections, and the small catches that prevent big bills.

Emergency Repair

Machine down mid-job? Unscheduled breakdowns jump the line. Call and we'll get rolling toward you.
